      Your data

      Cookie Lens stores counts, cookie names and domains, never cookie values.

      About the data

      How cookies are classified. Names are matched against the Open Cookie Database (Apache-2.0) for category and purpose. Domains are matched against Disconnect’s tracking protection list (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) for known trackers and their owners. The Public Suffix List (MPL-2.0) decides what counts as the same site. On top: third-party or not, lifetime, and whether the value looks like a unique ID. Values are read in memory and never saved.

      What it can’t know. Whether you clicked Accept on a banner, what a site does with a cookie on its servers, or tracking done without cookies (fingerprinting, local storage, login-based tracking). Unlisted cookies aren’t guessed at. A “tracker” label means a list or a strong signal says so, not proof of harm.

      Where your data goes. Nowhere. No servers, no analytics, no remote code. The lists ship inside the extension and are dated Oct 2026. Fonts: Instrument Serif, Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono (SIL OFL).
